## About Me

I turn experimental data into R&D decisions: what a chemistry can actually do, what it would cost to produce, and what its environmental footprint would be. PhD chemist with 15+ years in applied and sustainable chemistry, currently leading an R&D group at the Max Planck Institute of Colloids and Interfaces in Potsdam.

The products I've developed serve very different applications, and that's the point. Manganese micronutrient fertilizers designed for stability and bioavailability. Scale inhibitors for oil production, for Irkutsk Oil Company. An in-situ oil remediation technology for Nornickel, taken to a limited field deployment at the Norilsk spill site. Dust-binding agents for urban particulate control. Artificial humic substances from residual biomass by hydrothermal synthesis. Two patents came out of this work.

What connects them is how I work: I go into a problem someone else has defined, learn the system fast, and work out from the data what is worth pursuing and what isn't, before money goes into scale-up. Getting into an existing technology and finding where it can be improved is the part I enjoy most.

At the Max Planck Institute I run projects end to end: milestones, resources, an annual budget of up to €75k, lab operations and procurement, and an international team of two PhD students and a technician. I've co-supervised two doctoral theses to successful defense, and I'm used to being handed a goal rather than a to-do list.

Toolkit: analysis of experimental data, techno-economic assessment (TEA) and life-cycle assessment (LCA), with Python as my working language for data (including NOMspectra, an open-source tool I developed for high-resolution MS data). In the lab: organic and hydrothermal synthesis, green-chemistry protocols; NMR (1D/2D), mass spectrometry including FT-ICR-MS, XRD, SEM/TEM, HPLC/GC. I've been through scale-up, pilot and field trials on a few projects, enough to judge what the data has to show before that step is worth taking.

Where I want to go next: R&D and product development in specialty chemicals and agro/biostimulants, in a company with a technology I can get into and improve, or a new line I can take to prototype.

Based in Brandenburg (Berlin–Potsdam area). Permanent residency in Germany (Niederlassungserlaubnis), no visa sponsorship needed. Languages: English, German, Russian.
